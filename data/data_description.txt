elections_clean.csv: one row per single-union RC election, 15,270 rows x 21 columns.

fiscal_year: (ordinal) NLRB fiscal year of the monthly report the election appears in. 
A fiscal year runs from October to September (FY2012 = Oct 2011 - Sep 2012). 
Ordered from earliest to latest.

	2012
	2013
	...
	2026


case_number: (categorical) a unique string assigned by NLRB to identify each election case. 
format: region-case type-number (*)

	01-RC-216927
	...


case_name: (categorical) a unique descriptor assigned by NLRB to identify each election case (*)


employer: (categorical) the employer of which the union election takes place (*)


city: (categorical) the city in which the union election takes place (*)

state: (categorical) the state in which the union election takes place

	AL   alabama
	AK   alaska
	...
	WI   wisconsin
	WY   wyoming


census_region: (categorical) groups states into four geographic regions according to  U.S. Census Bureau conventions

	Northeast
	Midwest
	South
	West
	Territory

union_name: (categorical) name of union that the election is voted under (*)


parent_union: (categorical) parent union that the election union is part of

	Teamsters	International Brotherhood of Teamsters
	SEIU		Service Employees International Union, incl. 1199 locals
	IAM		International Association of Machinists and Aerospace Workers
	IUOE		International Union of Operating Engineers
	UFCW		United Food and Commercial Workers
	IBEW		International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers
	Workers United	Workers United (SEIU affiliate, kept as its own group)
	USW		United Steelworkers
	CWA		Communications Workers of America, incl. NewsGuild
	SPFPA		Security, Police and Fire Professionals of America
	UAW		United Auto Workers
	LIUNA		Laborers' International Union of North America
	AFSCME		American Federation of State, County and Municipal Employees
	Nurses		any union with "nurse" in its name
	IATSE		International Alliance of Theatrical Stage Employees
	UA (Plumbers)	United Association of plumbers, pipefitters and sprinkler fitters
	ATU		Amalgamated Transit Union
	UNITE HERE	UNITE HERE (hotel and food service workers)
	RWDSU		Retail, Wholesale and Department Store Union (UFCW affiliate, kept as its own group)
	SMART		Sheet Metal, Air, Rail and Transportation Workers
	AFT		American Federation of Teachers
	OPEIU		Office and Professional Employees International Union
	UGSOA		United Government Security Officers of America
	IUPAT		International Union of Painters and Allied Trades
	BCTGM		Bakery, Confectionery, Tobacco Workers and Grain Millers
	Carpenters	United Brotherhood of Carpenters
	UWUA		Utility Workers Union of America
	IAFF		International Association of Fire Fighters
	ILWU		International Longshore and Warehouse Union
	NATCA		National Air Traffic Controllers Association
	Ironworkers	International Association of Ironworkers
	TWU		Transport Workers Union
	Other		smaller and independent unions


eligible: (continuous) count of eligible voters in a union election


petition_date (yyyy-mm-dd): earliest date on which the union election is petitioned to the NLRB. 
If the docket lists none, the case file date from the monthly report is used instead. (*)


election_date (yyyy-mm-dd): date on which the union election takes place. 
Where the report left it blank, it is filled from the docket's first Tally of Ballots (*)


days_petition_to_election: (continuous) duration between election date and petition date, counted in days


n_participants: (continuous) number of parties and representatives who participated in the case (employer, union, lawyers, intervenors)


employer_has_lawyer: (binary categorical) whether the employer is supported by lawyers against the election

	1  the employer has lawyers
	0  the employer doesn't have lawyers


n_employer_lawyers: (continuous) number of lawyers the employer has against the election


employer_law_firms: (categorical) names of the employers' lawyers' law firms. Several values, separated by ;.


petitioner_has_lawyer: (binary categorical) whether the union (petitioner) is supported by lawyers for the election

	1  union supported by lawyers
	0  unions not supported by lawyers


n_petitioner_lawyers: (continuous) number of lawyers the union has for the election, as a count


has_intervenor: (binary categorical)  describes the presence of intervenors during the election

	1  has intervenors
	0  does not have intervenors


union_won: (binary categorical) [Target variable] describes the final official union election result based on vote counts

	1	win: Certification of Representative issued
	0	loss: Certification of Results issued


(*) are desriptive variables that will likely not be used in the ML pipeline
