fiscal_year: the year the union election takes place

	2012
	2013
	...
	2026


case_number: a unique string assigned by NLRB to identify each election case (*)


case_name: a unique descriptor assigned by NLRB to identify each election case (*)


employer: the employer of which the union election takes place (*)


city: the city in which the union election takes place (*)


state: the state in which the union election takes place

	AL   alabama
	AK   alaska
	...
	WI   wisconsin
	WY   wyoming


census_region: groups states into four geographic regions according to census conventions

	Northeast
	Midwest
	South
	West


union_name: name of union that the election is voted under (*)


parent_union: parent union that the election union is part of

	Teamsters    
	SEIU
	UFCW
	IBEW
	IAM
	USW
	UAW
	CWA
	IUOE
	LIUNA
	AFSCME
	SPFPA
	UNITE HERE
	AFT
	Nurses
	Carpenters
	IUPAT
	Other


eligible: count of eligible voters in a union election


petition_date (yyyy-mm-dd): date on which the union election is petitioned to the NLRB (*)


election_date (yyyy-mm-dd): date on which the union election takes place (*)


days_petition_to_election: duration between election date and petition date, counted in days


n_participants: count of voters who participated in the election


employer_has_lawyer: whether the employer is supported by lawyers against the election

	1  the employer has lawyers
	0  the employer doesn't have lawyers


n_employer_lawyers: number of lawyers the employer has against the election


employer_law_firms: names of the employers' lawyers' law firms (&)


petitioner_has_lawyer: whether the union is supported by lawyers for the election

	1  union supported by lawyers
	0  unions not supported by lawyers


n_petitioner_lawyers: number of lawyers the union has for the election


has_intervenor: describes the presence of intervenors during the election

	1  has intervenors
	0  does not have intervenors


union_won: describes the union election result based on vote counts

	1 	win; more than 50% of voters are for the union
    0 	lose; more than 50% of voters are against the union


(*) are desriptive variables that will likely not be used in the ML pipeline
